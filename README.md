THE TOP 200 RICHEST PERSON IN THE WORLD

## About Dataset
The Top 200 Richest World's Billionaires Dataset provides a detailed overview of the wealthiest individuals 
globally, according to the Forbes list. This dataset captures essential information about the top 200 
billionaires, offering insights into their financial standing, age, industry, and more. It is a valuable resource 
for analyzing trends in wealth distribution, industry dominance, and the demographics of the world's 
richest people.

## Column Descriptions
• Serial Number: Sequential numbering of the billionaires in the dataset.
• Rank: The global ranking of the billionaire based on their net worth as per the Forbes list.
• Name: The name of the billionaire.
• Age: The age of the billionaire.
• Country: The name of the country to which they belong.
• Net worth (In Billion Dollars): The total net worth of the billionaire, expressed in billions of dollars.
• Industry: The industry or sector to which the billionaire's wealth is primarily attributed.
